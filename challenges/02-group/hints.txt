`ls -l /opt/lab/ch02/build-notes.txt` shows two names: the owner
(you) and the group (`eng`). niko gets in through the group.
---
The nine permission letters are three groups of three: owner,
group, everyone else. You only need to change the middle three.
---
The group should be able to read, but not write. Read is 4.
Everyone else -- which is nini -- gets nothing.
---
    chmod 640 /opt/lab/ch02/build-notes.txt

6 for you (read + write), 4 for the `eng` group (read only),
0 for everybody else.
