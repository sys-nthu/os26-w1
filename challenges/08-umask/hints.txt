Try it in your current terminal first, before worrying about
making it stick:

    umask 0027
    touch ~/demo2.txt && ls -l ~/demo2.txt
---
Where do the digits come from? A new file starts at `666` and a
new folder at `777`. The umask digits are subtracted.

666 minus 027 is 640, which is `rw-r-----`. That is what you want.
---
The setting disappears when you close the terminal, because it
only applied to that one shell.

Add the line to the end of `~/.bashrc` so it runs every time:

    echo 'umask 0027' >> ~/.bashrc
---
