You are not allowed to touch the file. So look at everything else
in the picture.

    ls -ld /opt/lab/ch06/dropbox
---
When you delete something, what actually changes on disk? The
file's contents do not change -- the file stops existing. What
changed is the *list of names* in the folder.
---
Changing the list of names in a folder needs `w` on the folder.
nini has no `w` on `dropbox`.
---
    sudo chmod 777 /opt/lab/ch06/dropbox

`sudo chmod 757` works too, and so does putting the folder in a group
nini belongs to and using `775`.

The lesson: permission to delete a file has nothing to do with the
file. It lives on the folder, because removing a name is a change
to the folder.
