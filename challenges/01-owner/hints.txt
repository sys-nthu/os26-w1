Run `ls -l` on the file and read the first ten characters.

Every dash is something nobody is allowed to do. Right now they
are all dashes.
---
You want read and write for yourself, and nothing whatsoever for
anybody else. nini is "everybody else" here.
---
`chmod` takes three digits: one for you, one for your group, one
for everyone else.

read is 4, write is 2, execute is 1. Add up the ones you want.
---
    chmod 600 /opt/lab/ch01/notes.txt

6 is 4+2, read plus write, for you. 0 and 0 for the group and for
everyone else.
